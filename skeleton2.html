FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with fashion brands?

No. Fashion is the category represented most strongly in my current portfolio, but I can discuss Shopify projects from other e-commerce niches based on their requirements.

Do you build Shopify stores from scratch?

Yes. I build Shopify storefronts from the initial setup through design, development, and the agreed launch preparation.

Do you use premium themes or custom code?

I select a suitable theme for the project and customize it as needed. When the requirements call for additional functionality, I can implement code-based customizations. Theme licensing is separate from my development fee unless otherwise agreed.

Is the paid theme included in the price?

Paid theme licensing is separate from the development fee unless explicitly included in the proposal.

Do you add products?

Product setup can be included within an agreed quantity and scope. The number of products and the required data should be confirmed before the project begins.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on the project's complexity, catalog, required customizations, and the timely availability of content and feedback. I'll provide an estimated timeline in the proposal.

What are the payment terms?

My standard payment structure is 50% upfront and 50% upon delivery.

Are revisions included?

Revisions are defined by the agreed project scope. The proposal will clarify what is included.

Do you provide support after delivery?

I provide a handover when the project is completed. Any applicable bug-fix period and its conditions will be stated in the proposal. Ongoing maintenance is not automatically included.

How do I get a quote?

Send me a message on WhatsApp with a short description of your brand, products, target market, and what you need from your Shopify store.

Planning to Launch Your Shopify Store?

Tell me about your brand and what you need. We'll discuss the requirements and determine the right scope for your project.
